Why Loxahatchee & The Acreage

Equestrian Estates · Agricultural Land · West of Wellington

Loxahatchee and The Acreage are Palm Beach County's answer to country living — large parcels zoned for horses, agricultural use, and rural residential lifestyle, all within 30–45 minutes of Palm Beach Island. Buyers here value privacy, space, and freedom from the density of coastal communities.

Proximity to Wellington's world-class equestrian facilities makes this area a natural fit for horse owners who want their own property while staying close to the Global Dressage Festival, the Winter Equestrian Festival, and the Palm Beach International Equestrian Center.

Property Types in Loxahatchee

Equestrian Estates

$800K – $5M+

Full horse facilities — stables, paddocks, riding arenas, and pasture land. Zoned for horses with trail access. Perfect for serious equestrians who want their horses at home.

Agricultural Parcels

$300K – $2M+

Raw or partially improved land for farming, homesteading, or future development. Significant acreage available at prices unavailable anywhere near the coast.

Custom Estate Homes

$700K – $4M

Custom-built homes on large lots with room for pools, guest houses, workshops, and outbuildings. Complete privacy without the HOA restrictions of gated communities.

The Acreage Residential

$400K – $1.5M

Established residential community on 1.25-acre lots. Family-friendly, quiet streets, close to Acreage Community Park, and excellent value for the space you get.
