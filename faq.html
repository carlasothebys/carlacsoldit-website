Buying & Finance

5 questions
▼ What are closing costs in Florida?
Florida closing costs for buyers typically total 1–3% of the purchase price and include:

• Documentary stamps on the mortgage (0.35% of loan amount)
• Intangible tax (0.2% of loan)
• Owner's title insurance
• Lender's title insurance
• Attorney and settlement fees
• Recording fees
• Prorated property taxes and HOA dues

Sellers typically pay 5–6% in commissions plus documentary stamps on the deed (0.7% of sale price).
▼ How long does it take to close on a luxury home in Florida?
With financing: 30–60 days from executed contract. Cash closings can be completed in 10–14 days. For new construction, expect 3–12 months depending on construction stage. Carla's team accelerates timelines through relationships with local title companies and real estate attorneys.
▼ What is Florida homestead exemption?
Florida's homestead exemption reduces assessed property value by up to $50,000 for primary residences. Florida also has the Save Our Homes cap, limiting annual assessed value increases to 3% or CPI — whichever is less. This can translate to tens of thousands of dollars in annual property tax savings over time, especially in an appreciating market.
▼ What are typical HOA fees in Palm Beach County golf communities?
HOA fees vary significantly by community size and amenities.

Small, exclusive clubs (Bears Club, 92 homes): $2,000–$4,000/month
Mid-size communities (BallenIsles, Frenchman's Creek): $1,000–$2,500/month
Larger communities (Jonathan's Landing, Jupiter Country Club): $500–$1,200/month

Club dues are generally separate from HOA fees. Always request a 12-month HOA budget and reserve study before making an offer.
▼ Can non-US residents buy real estate in Florida?
Yes. Non-US residents and foreign nationals can purchase Florida real estate with few restrictions. Foreign buyers should be aware of FIRPTA (Foreign Investment in Real Property Tax Act), which requires withholding of up to 15% of the sale price at closing — this is a tax prepayment that can be reduced or refunded via IRS filing. Carla's team works with international buyers and coordinates FIRPTA-compliant closings regularly.

Market & Lifestyle

8 questions
▼ What is the Palm Beach County real estate market like in 2026?
The 2026 Palm Beach County luxury market remains exceptionally robust and supply-constrained. Median sale price in the $2M+ segment is up approximately 9% year-over-year. Inventory remains tight in Jupiter and Palm Beach Island. New construction is absorbing significant demand from high-tax state relocators. Days on market for correctly priced luxury homes averages 40–70 days.
▼ Is there a state income tax in Florida?
No. Florida has no state income tax, no inheritance tax, and no estate tax. For a household earning $1M+ annually relocating from New York, New Jersey, California, or Illinois, the annual tax savings can exceed $100,000–$200,000+. This is one of the primary drivers of Palm Beach County's sustained luxury demand.
▼ What is Palm Beach Island like for real estate?
Palm Beach Island is one of the most coveted real estate addresses in the world — a barrier island with ocean-to-lake lots, historic estates, Worth Avenue luxury shopping, and strict architectural preservation. Supply is permanently constrained by geography. Entry condos start around $1M; single-family estates range from $5M to $100M+.

Explore the Palm Beach Island real estate guide →
▼ What is Palm Beach Gardens known for?
Palm Beach Gardens is known for world-class golf communities (BallenIsles, Frenchman's Creek, Mirasol, Old Palm, Panther National), A-rated public schools, upscale retail at The Gardens Mall, proximity to PBI Airport, and a family-friendly yet sophisticated lifestyle. It is the largest land-area city in Palm Beach County.

Explore the Palm Beach Gardens luxury homes guide →
▼ What is Wellington FL known for in real estate?
Wellington is the winter equestrian capital of the world. It hosts the Palm Beach International Equestrian Center and the Global Dressage Festival, attracting equestrian enthusiasts and professionals from around the globe each January–April. Equestrian properties — from polo farm estates to equestrian-friendly gated communities — are the primary luxury market driver.
▼ What airports are near Palm Beach County?
Palm Beach International Airport (PBI) — primary commercial airport, 15–20 min from West Palm Beach, 25–35 min from Jupiter.
Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International (FLL) — 60–75 min south.
Miami International (MIA) — approx. 90 min south.
North Palm Beach County General Aviation Airport (F45) — executive/private aviation, closest to Jupiter.
▼ What are the best school districts in Palm Beach County?
Palm Beach Gardens and Jupiter are widely considered to have the best public schools in Palm Beach County — including Dwyer High, Jupiter High, and Palm Beach Gardens High. Top private schools: The Benjamin School, Palm Beach Day Academy, and The Weiss School. Wellington is also noted for equestrian-community schools.
