Why Palm Beach Gardens is South Florida's Premier Family Luxury Market

Palm Beach Gardens is the geographic heart of Palm Beach County's luxury golf corridor — home to more private country clubs per square mile than virtually any city in America. From BallenIsles (three courses, 22 tennis courts, 1,570 homes) to Panther National (Tiger Woods and Justin Thomas design, $3M–$20M homes), PBG offers a club lifestyle that is unmatched anywhere in South Florida.

The city's planned community design gives it an orderly, beautiful character that sets it apart from more organically developed South Florida cities. Wide boulevards, mature landscaping, and well-maintained communities create an environment that attracts families who want space, safety, and quality of life alongside luxury amenities.

PGA Boulevard is the city's luxury spine — lined with high-end retail, dining, and services that serve the surrounding club communities. Palm Beach Gardens Medical Center, Jupiter Medical Center (nearby), and access to world-class healthcare add another layer of appeal for second-home buyers and retirees.

The new construction pipeline for Palm Beach Gardens is among the most active in Florida — with Ritz-Carlton Residences, Alba Reserve, and Panther National all bringing new ultra-luxury inventory to a market that has historically been dominated by resale. This is creating exceptional pre-construction investment opportunities for buyers who move quickly with the right agent.

Palm Beach Gardens — Lifestyle, Culture & What Makes It Extraordinary

The Golf Capital of the World is much more than golf — here's the full picture.

Panther National — The New Crown Jewel

Panther National represents the most significant new luxury golf community in the United States. Co-designed by Tiger Woods and Justin Thomas — two of the sport's all-time greats — the course and community are being developed to an extraordinary standard. Homes range from $3M to $20M+. Membership is by invitation and inquiry only. The development is positioned as the definitive ultra-luxury golf address for the next generation of high-net-worth buyers who want something genuinely new rather than inheriting someone else's membership. Contact Carla — she has direct developer access for qualified buyers.

Honda Classic — PGA Tour in Your Backyard

The Honda Classic at PGA National Resort is one of the PGA Tour's marquee events — drawing the world's top golfers to Palm Beach Gardens every February. For PBG residents, this is not a travel destination; it is a neighborhood event. The Honda Classic has reinforced PBG's identity as the Golf Capital of the World and strengthened the community's connection to professional golf's highest level. Honda Classic week is one of the most exciting weeks of the year to be in Palm Beach Gardens.

PGA of America Headquarters

The PGA of America — the professional organization representing 28,000+ golf professionals — is headquartered in Palm Beach Gardens. This alone speaks to the city's centrality in global golf. Having the sport's governing body headquartered in your community means a constant flow of golf industry professionals, business connections, charitable events, and the ongoing prestige of being at the epicenter of the game that has defined this region for decades.

Frenchman's Creek — PBG's Most Exclusive Club

Frenchman's Creek Beach & Country Club is the undisputed crown jewel of Palm Beach Gardens' private clubs. With residents-only membership (no outside members), two championship courses, 13 tennis courts, a private beach club on the ocean, and a marina — all within one gated community — it offers the most complete lifestyle package of any club in the county. Initiation is $375,000 and annual dues are $53,447 — the highest in Palm Beach County, reflecting the unmatched lifestyle it provides.

Dining, Shopping & PGA Boulevard

PGA Boulevard is Palm Beach Gardens' luxury spine — lined with high-end retail, acclaimed restaurants, medical services, and everything a luxury lifestyle requires. Downtown at the Gardens, The Gardens Mall (one of South Florida's finest), and the expanding Alton Town Center give PBG a shopping and dining scene that few suburban communities can match. Avocado Grill, Coolinary Café, and Pistache are among the dining highlights. The city's planned character means traffic management is superior to most comparable Florida communities.

Schools, Healthcare & Family Life

PBG is consistently ranked among Palm Beach County's best school zones. Palm Beach Gardens Community High School earns A ratings. Private options include The Benjamin School, Palm Beach Gardens Day School, and Oxbridge Academy. Palm Beach Gardens Medical Center and proximity to Jupiter Medical Center provide world-class healthcare. These quality-of-life attributes, combined with the club lifestyle, make PBG the most sought-after destination for relocating Northeast families with children — a demographic that drove enormous demand for BallenIsles and Mirasol throughout 2023–2026.

Why Palm Beach Gardens is the Smart Money's Choice in 2026

Palm Beach Gardens offers something no other market in South Florida can replicate: the deepest concentration of private golf clubs (8+ world-class options), the strongest school system in the county, planned community infrastructure that protects quality of life, and a new construction pipeline (Panther National, Ritz-Carlton Residences, Alba Reserve) that is drawing a new generation of elite buyers.
