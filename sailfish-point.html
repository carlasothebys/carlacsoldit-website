About Sailfish Point

Hutchinson Island · Stuart, Martin County FL

Sailfish Point occupies the entire southern tip of Hutchinson Island — a 532-acre gated peninsula where the Atlantic Ocean and Indian River Lagoon meet. The result is something genuinely rare: ocean-to-river estates with water on multiple sides, private beach access, and deepwater marina access all within one guard-gated enclave with a single point of entry.

A private Arnold Palmer-designed championship golf course, full-service yacht club with slips for vessels up to 125 feet, tennis, fitness, a resort pool, and a private ocean beach club create a fully self-contained luxury lifestyle. Just one mile from the St. Lucie Inlet, residents are minutes from the Gulf Stream and some of the East Coast's finest offshore fishing for sailfish, mahi, wahoo, and tuna.

Despite being one of Florida's most prestigious private communities, Sailfish Point often represents exceptional value compared to equivalent Palm Beach County properties — making it a compelling choice for buyers who want more estate for their dollar without sacrificing an ounce of privacy or prestige.
