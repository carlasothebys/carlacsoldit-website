Why Ocean Ridge

Private Barrier Island · Direct Atlantic Ocean · Boynton Beach Area

Ocean Ridge is one of Palm Beach County's best-kept secrets. Situated on a narrow barrier island just south of Manalapan and north of Briny Breezes, it's a residential-only community with no commercial development — just beautiful oceanfront and Intracoastal homes, wide streets, and total quiet.

Unlike busier Palm Beach communities, Ocean Ridge has no hotels, no restaurants, and no foot traffic. Residents here value absolute privacy, direct beach access, and the character of a genuine small coastal town. With only about 1,800 residents, the market is tight and properties move quickly when they appear.

Ocean Ridge Property Types

Oceanfront Estates

$5M – $25M+

Direct Atlantic Ocean frontage with private beach. Architecturally diverse — from classic Florida ranch-style to contemporary new construction. Rare and highly coveted.

Intracoastal Waterfront

$2M – $10M+

Stunning Intracoastal Waterway frontage with private docks. Boat access to the Lake Worth Lagoon and ocean via Boynton Beach Inlet. Beautiful sunset views.

Interior Estate Lots

$1.5M – $5M

Large interior lots with room for pools, guest houses, and tropical landscaping. Walking distance to the beach. Exceptional privacy without the oceanfront premium.

New Construction

$3M – $15M+

Contemporary custom homes being built throughout the community — modern architecture, smart-home technology, and resort-style amenities on private lots.
