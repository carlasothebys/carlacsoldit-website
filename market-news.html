Jupiter Florida Real Estate: Why Demand Remains Strong in 2026

Jupiter remains one of Florida's most sought-after luxury markets. Inventory remains tight, and demand continues from high-net-worth buyers relocating from New York, California, and internationally. Waterfront homes on the Loxahatchee River and Intracoastal are attracting serious buyer interest. Golf community properties in Admirals Cove, Bears Club and Loxahatchee Club continue to command premium pricing.

Golf Communities

2026 — Private Golf Club Report

Private Golf Club Membership Fees 2026: What Has Changed and What You Must Know Before Buying

Palm Beach County's private golf and country club membership fees have increased significantly over the past three years, driven by surging demand from new residents relocating from the Northeast and internationally. Many clubs now have waiting lists exceeding 2 years. Admirals Cove initiation fees range $285K-$475K. The Bears Club bond sits at $350K. The Loxahatchee Club requires $250K. Understanding the full cost - initiation, annual dues, trail fees, food minimums, and equity refund policies - is critical before buying in any club community.
