Why Singer Island Belongs in Every Serious Buyer's Conversation

Riviera Beach · Palm Beach County · Atlantic Ocean Direct Access

Singer Island is one of Palm Beach County's best-kept luxury secrets. This narrow barrier island — bounded by the Atlantic Ocean to the east and the Lake Worth Lagoon to the west — offers direct oceanfront living at price points that Palm Beach Island cannot touch. For buyers whose primary motivation is waking up to the Atlantic Ocean, Singer Island delivers that experience at 50–70% of the cost.

The island has undergone a significant luxury evolution since 2018. New high-rise towers with resort-caliber amenities have raised the bar for oceanfront condo living here, while a small collection of single-family oceanfront estates on the island's quieter northern end cater to buyers who want direct beach access with full privacy. The result is a market that serves both the ultra-luxury buyer and the serious oceanfront buyer entering at the $800K–$2M tier.

Lost Tree Club — anchored at the southern tip of Singer Island near North Palm Beach — elevates the island's prestige dramatically. As one of Florida's most invitation-only private communities and the long-time home club of Jack Nicklaus, Lost Tree's presence puts Singer Island in a class of its own among Palm Beach County barrier islands below Jupiter Island.

Singer Island's location makes it an ideal base for buyers who want both Atlantic Ocean access and convenient proximity to Palm Beach County's best amenities. Palm Beach Island is a 15-minute drive south. Palm Beach International Airport is 20 minutes. The Gardens Mall and PGA Boulevard are 15 minutes west. And North Palm Beach's marina community, restaurants, and country club are literally next door.

Blue Heron Bridge — located at the southern tip of Singer Island at Phil Foster Park — is internationally recognized as one of the world's top shore-diving destinations. Scuba divers from around the globe visit specifically for the extraordinary marine life, artificial reef, and current-diving opportunities accessible directly from the island. This unique amenity adds a cultural and recreational dimension that few oceanfront communities in America can match.

The Value Proposition: A direct oceanfront 2BR/2BA unit on Singer Island at $1.2M–$2M offers the same Atlantic views and beach access as a $4M–$6M Palm Beach Island condo. For buyers who prioritize the ocean experience over the social address, Singer Island is the most rational choice in Palm Beach County.

Lost Tree Club — Singer Island's Crown Jewel

Invitation Only · Founded 1958 · Jack Nicklaus's Private Community

Lost Tree Club occupies a singular position in Palm Beach County's luxury hierarchy. Founded in 1958 on the southern tip of Singer Island where it meets North Palm Beach, it was designed from its inception as a completely private, self-contained community — no public access, no guest access, no visibility from the outside world. The club is member-owned, meaning it is governed entirely by its residents with no developer, no outside management company, and no commercial pressure.

The membership is intentionally small — fewer than several hundred families — and the selection process reflects that intimacy. Prospective members must be sponsored by two existing members who vouch personally for their character and compatibility with the community. Even then, acceptance requires a full membership vote. No amount of money can substitute for this process.

Jack Nicklaus — the greatest golfer in history — has been among Lost Tree's most celebrated members for decades. His continued connection to both North Palm Beach (where Nicklaus Design is headquartered) and the Lost Tree Club community underscores the caliber of the membership. He is joined by an undisclosed collection of CEOs, family office principals, retired political figures, and multigenerational wealth families who have found in Lost Tree what no other Florida community offers: complete, total privacy with world-class golf and a private ocean beach.

Why Singer Island Is an Exceptional Investment in 2026: Three structural dynamics drive Singer Island's investment case: (1) Supply is permanently limited — the island is a finite barrier island with no new land to develop, meaning every new luxury unit added competes against a shrinking pool of buildable sites; (2) Demand is accelerating as the broader Palm Beach County luxury migration creates spillover buyers who can't access Palm Beach Island at their budget; and (3) The value gap versus Palm Beach Island has historically narrowed over time — early Singer Island buyers have historically captured meaningful appreciation as the island upgrades.

Singer Island Lifestyle & Amenities

Ocean, golf, diving, dining — everything that makes this island extraordinary
🌊
Atlantic Ocean Beaches
Singer Island's Atlantic beaches are among Palm Beach County's finest — Blue Flag certified for water quality, clean, uncrowded, and backed by gentle dunes. Direct beach access from virtually every building. The island's smaller scale means beaches never feel crowded compared to the more commercial strips further south. Sunrise over the Atlantic from a Singer Island ocean tower is genuinely spectacular.
🤿
Blue Heron Bridge — World-Class Diving
Phil Foster Park and Blue Heron Bridge are internationally recognized as one of the world's top shore-diving sites. Voted top dive destination in multiple surveys, the bridge's current-driven dives reveal extraordinary marine life — seahorses, frogfish, nudibranchs, manatees, sea turtles. Dive magazine regularly ranks it among the world's best. This unique attraction draws a sophisticated global community to Singer Island year-round.
⛳
Golf — Lost Tree & Beyond
Lost Tree Club's private championship course is Singer Island's most prestigious golf option. Beyond Lost Tree, Old Port Cove's full marina community is next door, and the broader Palm Beach County golf corridor — Bears Club, Admirals Cove, BallenIsles, PGA National — is all within 20–35 minutes. Singer Island buyers enjoy world-class golf proximity without the inland commute that Golf Corridor addresses require.
⚓
Boating — Old Port Cove Next Door
Singer Island's western Intracoastal properties have direct dock capability on the Lake Worth Lagoon. Old Port Cove Marina in adjacent North Palm Beach is moments away — offering deepwater slips for 100ft+ yachts, full marina services, and quick Palm Beach Inlet access. For boaters, the combination of ocean access (Palm Beach Inlet) and protected Intracoastal is among the best in Palm Beach County.
🍽️
Dining & Entertainment
Singer Island's own dining scene includes waterfront options near Phil Foster Park. But the island's proximity to North Palm Beach — Waterway Café, Spoto's Oyster Bar, E.R. Bradley's — and the broader Palm Beach corridor means world-class dining is always close. Palm Beach Island's legendary restaurant scene (Café Boulud, Buccan, Meat Market) is 15 minutes south. PBG dining corridor is 15 min northwest.
🏖️
John D. MacArthur Beach State Park
One of Florida's finest state parks adjoins the north end of Singer Island — offering pristine undeveloped barrier island coastline, birding, kayaking through mangrove tunnels, and a nature center. The park's protected 535 acres of coastal habitat ensure that Singer Island's northern shore will never see commercial development, permanently protecting the residential community's natural character and views.
🏫
Schools & Education
Singer Island falls within the Palm Beach County School District with access to highly rated public schools. The Benjamin School (Palm Beach Gardens, 15 min) and Palm Beach Day Academy (15 min south) are the most sought-after private K–12 options nearby. The proximity to Jupiter Medical Center and Palm Beach Gardens Medical Center addresses healthcare needs.
✈️
Transportation & Access
Palm Beach International Airport (PBI) is 20 minutes south — offering direct flights to New York, Chicago, Boston, London, and major hubs. Fort Lauderdale International (FLL) is 45 minutes south. Miami International (MIA) is 75 minutes. The Brightline high-speed rail connects West Palm Beach to Miami, Fort Lauderdale, and Orlando — with the West Palm station 20 minutes from Singer Island.

Who Buys on Singer Island

The buyer profile driving this market's evolution

Singer Island attracts a sophisticated and diverse buyer pool who share one thing: they want the direct oceanfront Atlantic experience as the foundation of their lifestyle — and they understand the extraordinary value that Singer Island delivers compared to Palm Beach Island.

  • Finance executives & relocated Wall Street: Palm Beach County's Wall Street South migration produces buyers who want beachfront luxury but can't justify Palm Beach Island's premium for a secondary residence.
  • International buyers: European, Canadian, and Latin American buyers seeking direct Atlantic Ocean access, Florida tax benefits, and a year-round resort lifestyle. Singer Island's international appeal is strong.
  • Serious divers & ocean enthusiasts: Blue Heron Bridge's world-class diving draws a global buyer community specifically to Singer Island — a unique demand driver no other Palm Beach County address can replicate.
  • Retirees from high-tax states: New York, New Jersey, Connecticut, and Illinois retirees seeking oceanfront luxury, zero state tax, and a manageable scale of community. Singer Island's compact character appeals strongly to this demographic.
