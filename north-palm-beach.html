Why North Palm Beach Is Palm Beach County's Best-Kept Secret

The Village of North Palm Beach · Lake Worth Lagoon · Palm Beach Inlet Access

North Palm Beach sits in a geographic sweet spot that no other community in Palm Beach County can match. Positioned between Jupiter to the north and Palm Beach Island to the south, residents enjoy 15-minute access to world-class golf (Bears Club, Admirals Cove), quick proximity to Palm Beach Island's cultural and social scene, and an active boating corridor on the Lake Worth Lagoon — without paying the ultra-premium prices of either neighbor.

The Village of North Palm Beach was incorporated in 1956 and has maintained a carefully planned residential character ever since. Its waterfront parks, recreational facilities, and community design reflect a town that has always placed quality of life above density. The result: a genuine luxury community with a human scale that larger cities can never replicate.

The Intracoastal Waterway runs the full length of the community, creating an abundance of deep-water waterfront properties with private dock capability. The Palm Beach Inlet — just minutes south — provides direct Atlantic Ocean access for serious boaters. This combination of protected water living and quick ocean egress is rare in South Florida.

The "Jack Nicklaus Effect" on North Palm Beach cannot be overstated. The Golden Bear — winner of 18 major championships and widely considered the greatest golfer of all time — chose North Palm Beach as his home and established Nicklaus Design's global headquarters here. His presence created a gravitational pull for golf industry executives, fellow professionals, and wealthy enthusiasts who want proximity to the most accomplished name in golf course design.

Old Port Cove is the community's signature address: a gated marina enclave with deepwater dockage capable of handling yachts over 100 feet, full marina services, Intracoastal views, and the intimate atmosphere of a private club. NPB Country Club adds golf and tennis amenities to an already remarkable lifestyle package. And just across the bridge, Lost Tree Club — one of Florida's most invitation-only private communities — anchors the area's elite credentials.

Value vs. Neighbors: A comparable $3M waterfront property in North Palm Beach would price at $5M+ on Palm Beach Island. The same lifestyle quality at 40–60% of the cost. That's why finance executives and professional athletes from Jupiter to Palm Beach all consider NPB seriously.

Jack Nicklaus — North Palm Beach's Golden Legacy

18 Major Championships · Nicklaus Design HQ · The Golden Bear's Home

Jack William Nicklaus — nicknamed "The Golden Bear" — is universally regarded as the greatest golfer of all time. His record of 18 professional major championships remains unmatched. He turned professional in 1961 and chose North Palm Beach as his home — a choice that has defined the community's identity for six decades.

Nicklaus Design, his golf course architecture firm, is headquartered in North Palm Beach. The company has designed or redesigned over 400 golf courses on six continents — from Augusta National renovations to royal courses in the Middle East — making North Palm Beach the operational headquarters of golf's most influential design legacy. Executives, industry partners, and wealthy golf enthusiasts regularly visit the headquarters, and many have relocated to the area to maintain proximity to the firm.

Nicklaus and his wife Barbara have been among North Palm Beach's most visible and beloved community figures for decades. Their philanthropic contributions to Jupiter Medical Center, Florida schools, and youth programs have made the Nicklaus name synonymous with North Palm Beach generosity. The Nicklaus Children's Health Care Foundation — which Barbara champions — has delivered over $50 million in support to children's hospitals nationwide.

What Is Lost Tree Club?

Lost Tree Club is a private, invitation-only residential community and country club situated on Singer Island adjacent to North Palm Beach. Founded in 1958, it encompasses a self-contained enclave of estate homes, a private oceanfront beach, a championship golf course, and club facilities that have served as a private retreat for some of Florida's most accomplished residents. The community is member-owned and governed — meaning there is no developer, no outside management, and no public-facing commercial component.

⛳ Jack Nicklaus · Selective Membership · Off-Market

Who Belongs to Lost Tree?

Jack Nicklaus is Lost Tree's most celebrated member — his membership here is a natural extension of his decades-long North Palm Beach residency. Beyond Nicklaus, the membership has historically included CEOs of Fortune 500 companies, prominent attorneys, successful entrepreneurs, and retired political figures who seek genuine privacy without the visibility of Palm Beach Island's social circuit. Membership requires sponsorship by two existing members and an approval vote — money alone cannot buy entry.

🏠 $3M–$20M+ · Rarely Available · Always Off-Market

Lost Tree Real Estate

Lost Tree Club properties are among the most tightly held in Palm Beach County. The community comprises a limited number of estate lots — oceanfront, Intracoastal-facing, and interior — that rarely change hands. When they do, transactions happen almost exclusively through agent-to-agent communication, estate attorney referrals, or direct member networks — never through public listing. Lifestyle, Dining & Recreation

Everything that makes North Palm Beach uniquely livable
⛳
Golf — Multiple World-Class Options
NPB Country Club (municipal, excellent value), Lost Tree Club (private, invitation-only), plus quick access north to Bears Club ($500K+ initiation, Jack Nicklaus-designed), Admirals Cove (golf + marina), and Jonathan's Landing — all within 20 minutes. Jack Nicklaus's own headquarters means golf industry connections are woven into daily life.
⚓
Boating — Old Port Cove Marina
Old Port Cove Marina offers deepwater slips for vessels 100ft+, fuel dock, ship's store, and waterfront dining. The Palm Beach Inlet — just minutes south — provides direct Atlantic access. The Lake Worth Lagoon offers miles of protected water for day cruising, fishing, and water sports. Considered one of the finest boating communities in Palm Beach County.
🍽️
Dining — Village to Waterfront
Waterway Café (Intracoastal dining, local institution), Spoto's Oyster Bar (seafood, Palm Beach County landmark), Careyes (Old Port Cove, waterfront Mexican), E.R. Bradley's Saloon (waterfront bar), and the nearby PGA Boulevard corridor adds dozens more options in Palm Beach Gardens just 10 minutes away.
🏖️
Beaches — Public & Private
North Palm Beach offers easy access to Singer Island's Atlantic beaches — among the cleanest and least crowded in Palm Beach County. Lost Tree Club members enjoy a private oceanfront beach. The Village of North Palm Beach maintains excellent public beach park facilities. Ocean access via Palm Beach Inlet is a quick boat ride for deep-water fishing and offshore cruising.
🏫
Schools — Top-Rated Palm Beach County
North Palm Beach is served by highly rated Palm Beach County public schools. Private options nearby include The Benjamin School (Pre-K through 12, one of South Florida's finest), Palm Beach Day Academy, and Oxbridge Academy of the Palm Beaches (STEM-focused, IB program). Benjamin School's proximity and reputation make NPB a top choice for families.
🏥
Healthcare — Jupiter Medical Center
Jupiter Medical Center — Palm Beach County's top-rated community hospital — is 15 minutes north. Palm Beach Gardens Medical Center is 10 minutes west. Several world-class specialty practices (cardiology, orthopedic, concierge medicine) serve the area's affluent residential community, with many physicians maintaining practices within the village itself.
🎾
Tennis & Fitness
NPB Country Club offers multiple clay and hard courts. The area's private clubs add significant tennis infrastructure. The Maltz Jupiter Theatre, Loggerhead Marinelife Center (sea turtle research and rehabilitation), and MacArthur Beach State Park all add cultural and nature dimensions to an already rich lifestyle.
🛍️
Shopping — PBG & Worth Avenue Proximity
The Gardens Mall (Palm Beach Gardens, 10 min) and Alton Town Center bring premium retail within easy reach. Worth Avenue on Palm Beach Island — America's most prestigious shopping street — is 20 minutes south. The combination of local village shopping and world-class luxury retail within easy reach is unmatched in this price range.
