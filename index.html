Median Sale Price — Jupiter / Tequesta ($K)

Days on Market — Jupiter / Tequesta

Jupiter / Tequesta 2026 Outlook: The Jupiter luxury market remains one of Florida's most resilient. Waterfront inventory is at historic lows — less than 60 days of supply. Bears Club and Admirals Cove are attracting multiple-offer situations on competitively priced homes. Northeast relocation demand (New York, Connecticut, New Jersey) remains elevated. New construction land in Jupiter is essentially exhausted, creating a structural supply floor that will support prices long-term. Cash transactions dominate above $4M. Off-market sales account for 25–30% of $5M+ Jupiter closings.

Median Sale Price — Palm Beach County ($K)

Days on Market — Palm Beach County

Palm Beach County 2026 Outlook: Palm Beach County is experiencing a once-in-a-generation transformation. The arrival of Goldman Sachs, Citadel, Apollo, and hundreds of family offices has shifted PBC from a retirement market to one of the most active luxury markets in America. $2B+ in new luxury residential construction is underway along South Flagler Drive and in Palm Beach Gardens. Median prices are up 8.2% YoY in the luxury segment. The county's no-state-income-tax advantage continues to attract $1M+ earners from New York and California at record rates.

Median Sale Price — Martin County ($K)

Days on Market — Martin County

Martin County 2026 Outlook: Martin County — home to Jupiter Island (America's wealthiest zip code, 33455), Hobe Sound, Stuart, and Sewall's Point — is experiencing renewed demand from buyers priced out of Palm Beach County's most competitive corridors. Jupiter Island median prices exceed $8M and available inventory remains critically tight. Martin County's "no overdevelopment" ethos (strict growth controls) means supply will never catch up to demand. The Sailfish Point community on Hutchinson Island remains one of Florida's best-kept secrets for golf + ocean + Intracoastal living. Martin County median home prices rose 6.1% YoY in 2025.

Luxury Sales Volume — $2M+ Closings per Quarter

Buyer Origin — Luxury Segment

Luxury $2M+ Segment Outlook: The $2M+ luxury market in Palm Beach County is structurally undersupplied. Private golf communities (Bears Club, Admirals Cove, Old Palm, Frenchman's Creek) are seeing sub-45-day inventory. International buyer activity — particularly from Brazil, Canada, and the UK — remains elevated. Buyer profiles: finance executives from NY/CT/MA/IL relocating permanently; PGA Tour professionals and athletes; tech entrepreneurs from California; Brazilian and Latin American UHNW investors. Zero state income tax saves a $2M earner $200K+ annually versus New York — making even a $10M home acquisition cash-flow-positive within 5 years versus comparable Northeast living.
