Why Manalapan

South Ocean Blvd · Florida's Most Exclusive Address

Manalapan is a town of fewer than 500 residents — and one of the wealthiest zip codes in the United States. Located on a narrow barrier island between the Atlantic Ocean and Lake Worth Lagoon, just south of Palm Beach Island, Manalapan offers something Palm Beach itself cannot: true compound-size lots spanning from ocean to lake, with complete privacy and no through traffic.

Properties here are generational trophy assets. Many have never appeared on MLS. Sales are conducted privately between trusted advisors. Carla's membership in ONE Sotheby's Sports & Entertainment Division and her 29-year network of ultra-high-net-worth relationships makes her uniquely positioned to represent buyers and sellers in this market.
