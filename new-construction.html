Market Context

Why Palm Beach County New Construction Is Booming in 2025–2027

West Palm Beach has quietly become one of the most competitive luxury real estate markets in the United States. Here's why the world's wealthiest buyers are choosing Palm Beach County over Manhattan, Miami, and Los Angeles:

🏦

Finance Hub Migration

Goldman Sachs, Apollo, Citadel, Blackstone, and hundreds of family offices have relocated to or expanded in Palm Beach County — bringing a permanent wave of ultra-high-net-worth demand.

🌴

No State Income Tax

Florida's zero state income tax saves high earners $500K–$2M+ per year vs. New York or California. For ultra-wealthy buyers, a $3M condo saves more than its price in 2–3 years of tax savings.

🏗️

Supply Constraints

Palm Beach Island has virtually no buildable land. West Palm Beach's Flagler Drive corridor has only a handful of sites remaining — new ultra-luxury supply is permanently constrained, supporting appreciation.

✈️

PBI Airport Access

Palm Beach International Airport (PBI) offers direct flights to New York, Boston, Chicago, and LA. Private aviation at KOBE (North Palm Beach) and KSUA (Stuart) serves Jupiter & the Treasure Coast.

⛳

World-Class Golf

20+ private golf & country clubs within 20 miles — Admirals Cove, Bears Club, BallenIsles, Mirasol, Old Palm, Trump National, and the newest: Panther National co-designed by Tiger Woods & Justin Thomas.

🌊

Waterfront Lifestyle

The Intracoastal Waterway, Atlantic Ocean beaches, and the Jupiter Inlet give Palm Beach County an unmatched waterfront lifestyle — boating, fishing, paddleboarding, and beach access year-round.

Buyer's Guide

Pre-Construction Buying: What You Need to Know

Buying a luxury pre-construction condo is different from a resale purchase. Here's your insider guide from someone who has represented buyers in dozens of new development transactions in Palm Beach County.

✅ Advantages of Pre-Construction

  • Lock in today's price — deliver at tomorrow's value
  • Choose your floor, unit, and finishes
  • Staged deposits over construction period (vs. 100% at closing)
  • Brand-new construction — no deferred maintenance
  • First buyer often gets the best units at the best prices
  • Developer concessions (upgrades, parking, storage) more negotiable at pre-launch

⚠️ What to Watch For

  • Developer contracts are one-sided — always use a buyer's agent
  • Delivery date delays are common — build in flexibility
  • Confirm what's included vs. upgrade costs
  • Understand the HOA/condo fee structure before contracting
  • Verify developer's track record and financial backing
  • Have your financing or proof of funds ready — developers move fast
