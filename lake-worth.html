Why Lake Worth

Historic Estates · Arts District · Intracoastal Waterfront

Lake Worth Beach (officially renamed from Lake Worth) is one of Palm Beach County's most character-rich communities. Where Palm Beach Island is polished and formal, Lake Worth is authentic and eclectic — with a thriving arts scene, independent restaurants, and one of the county's most active beach communities.

For real estate buyers, Lake Worth offers exceptional value. Oceanfront and Intracoastal properties here are significantly less expensive than comparable properties on Palm Beach Island, yet offer the same beautiful water and sunrises. Historic homes, mid-century architecture, and new luxury construction all coexist here.

Lake Worth Neighborhoods

Lake Worth Beach Oceanfront

$2M – $10M+

Direct Atlantic Ocean frontage — historic and renovated estate homes with private beach access. Significant value compared to Palm Beach Island oceanfront.

Intracoastal Waterway

$800K – $5M+

Waterfront homes and condos on the Lake Worth Lagoon with private docks, beautiful sunset views, and direct Intracoastal access.

Historic District

$400K – $2M

Beautifully preserved Mediterranean revival and Craftsman homes. Walking distance to the beach, downtown restaurants, galleries, and the historic Lake Worth Playhouse.

College Park & Surrounding

$350K – $1.5M

Established residential neighborhoods with mature tree canopy, charming streets, and proximity to Palm Beach International Airport.
