Who Lives in These Communities? Notable Residents

Palm Beach County's private golf clubs attract the world's most successful athletes, executives, and entertainers. Here's a club-by-club look at the celebrity and notable-resident profiles that define each community.

The Bears Club · Jupiter

92-Home Ultra-Exclusive Enclave

The Bears Club's 92-home limit, Jack Nicklaus–designed course, and absolute privacy make it Jupiter's most coveted address for elite buyers — including PGA Tour professionals, professional athletes, and ultra-high-net-worth individuals who require genuine discretion.

Homes: $5M–$30M+ · Membership: Required · Outside Members: Allowed

Admirals Cove · Jupiter

891 Homes · Deep-Water Marina

Admirals Cove is Jupiter's largest private golf and marina community and one of its most celebrity-friendly — the combination of a 63-slip deep-water marina (accommodating yachts to approximately 130 feet), 24-hour security, and a full-service country club attracts professional athletes, finance executives, and entertainment figures who want a private waterfront lifestyle with genuine amenities.

Homes: $1.5M–$20M+ · Marina · 891 Homes · Golf Required

Panther National · Palm Beach Gardens

PGA Tour's Newest Address

Co-designed by Tiger Woods and Justin Thomas, Panther National is already attracting PGA Tour professionals, sports figures, and ultra-high-net-worth buyers as Palm Beach Gardens' most exciting new address. Membership by invitation only — $3M–$20M+ homes.

New Construction · By Invitation Only · $3M–$20M+

Jupiter Hills Club · Jupiter

Invitation-Only · Tom Fazio Masterpiece

Considered one of Florida's finest pure golf experiences — two Tom Fazio–designed courses, fewer than 300 members, and a strictly private, invitation-only membership. Fees are not publicly disclosed. Jupiter Hills Club is the gold standard of discretion in Palm Beach County private golf.

Invitation Only · Fees Undisclosed · 2 Tom Fazio Courses

BallenIsles · Palm Beach Gardens

1,570 Homes · PGA Tour Proximity

BallenIsles' proximity to PGA National (home of The Honda Classic) and Panther National makes it a popular choice for PGA Tour players and their families who want the full club lifestyle — 3 courses, 22 tennis courts, and resort amenities — at a more accessible price point than Bears Club or Jupiter Hills.

1,570 Homes · $295K Equity · 80% Refund to Seller

Frenchman's Creek · Palm Beach Gardens

Residents Only · Private Beach Club

Frenchman's Creek is the most self-contained luxury community in Palm Beach County — no outside members, private oceanfront beach club, marina, two golf courses, and the highest annual dues ($53,447). It attracts residents who want everything within the gates and maximum exclusivity. A favorite among prominent families from the Northeast.

606 Homes · No Outside Members · Private Beach · Marina
