Why Stuart & Sewall's Point

Treasure Coast · Private Peninsula Living · World-Class Fishing

Stuart is the county seat of Martin County — a charming waterfront city that has carefully managed its growth to maintain an authentic Florida character. No high-rises, no overdevelopment. Sewall's Point is an exclusive peninsula community surrounded by the St. Lucie River and the Indian River Lagoon — one of Florida's most biologically diverse waterways.

Sailfish Point, at the southern tip of Hutchinson Island, adds a gated oceanfront community with a private yacht club and golf course. The area attracts buyers who want serious waterfront living — deep-water dockage, ocean access, and total privacy — at prices well below Palm Beach Island.

Stuart & Sewall's Point Communities

Sewall's Point Peninsula

$1.5M – $15M+

Exclusive peninsula community entirely surrounded by water. Large lots, private docks, lush tropical landscaping, and complete privacy. One of Martin County's most coveted addresses.

Downtown Stuart Waterfront

$800K – $5M

Walkable waterfront living with charming restaurants, boutiques, and a vibrant arts scene. Riverfront condos and homes with easy marina access.

St. Lucie River Estates

$1M – $8M+

Deep-water estate properties along the St. Lucie River with direct ocean access through the St. Lucie Inlet. World-class fishing right from your dock.

Rocky Point

$700K – $4M

Waterfront community on the North Fork of the St. Lucie River — a hidden gem with deep-water dockage, quiet streets, and outstanding value relative to Palm Beach County.
