About Hobe Sound

Martin County · 33455 · Where Jupiter Island begins

Hobe Sound is an unincorporated community in Martin County, Florida, nestled between the Atlantic Ocean, the Indian River Lagoon, and the Loxahatchee River. It shares a zip code (33455) with Jupiter Island and borders it directly to the north. The Hobe Sound National Wildlife Refuge — 1,035 acres of pristine barrier island beach — ensures that development pressure remains minimal and the natural character of this coastline is permanently protected.

Despite its proximity to Jupiter and Palm Beach County, Hobe Sound feels worlds away in atmosphere. There is no significant commercial strip, no hotel district, and no tourism infrastructure. What you find instead is deep-water dockage, private golf, world-class fishing, preserved mangroves, and some of the most beautiful Intracoastal estate properties in Florida — at prices often 20–40% below equivalent Jupiter Island properties.

For buyers who want privacy, authenticity, waterfront access, and natural beauty — without paying Jupiter Island prices — Hobe Sound is one of Florida's best-kept real estate secrets.

Famous Residents & Notable Connections

Who calls Hobe Sound home — and why the world's most private figures choose it

Hobe Sound is one of Florida's most deliberately low-profile communities. Its famous residents are rarely photographed or publicized here — that's precisely the point. The combination of the National Wildlife Refuge, the absence of a commercial tourist strip, and a strong local culture of privacy makes it a destination for people who are serious about not being found. Unlike Jupiter — which has become famous for its celebrity residents — Hobe Sound guards its residents' identities with an almost institutional seriousness.

Golf Legend · Course Designer · Hobe Sound Icon

Greg Norman — "The Great White Shark"

Two-time Open Championship winner (1986, 1993) and one of golf's all-time legends, Greg Norman has a deep, longstanding connection to Hobe Sound. He co-designed The Medalist Golf Club alongside the late Pete Dye — creating one of the world's most exclusive private golf addresses in his adopted hometown. Norman's lifestyle has always reflected a preference for raw natural beauty and genuine privacy over the polished, commercialized golf resort experience. Hobe Sound — with its mangroves, the Indian River Lagoon, and miles of undeveloped barrier island beach — is exactly the kind of place Norman gravitates toward. Beyond golf, Norman has built a global brand around premium lifestyle products, wine, and real estate — and Hobe Sound's understated luxury perfectly embodies his philosophy.

PGA Tour Elite · Private Training · Invitation Only

The Medalist — Elite Training Ground

The Medalist Golf Club in Hobe Sound is widely recognized as one of the premier practice and training destinations for PGA Tour professionals. Co-designed by Greg Norman and Pete Dye, it is celebrated for its unmatched course conditioning, difficulty, and the absolute privacy it affords its members. Elite golfers preparing for major championships have long made The Medalist part of their competitive preparation. The world's top players choosing this quiet Martin County club as their training sanctuary speaks volumes about what Hobe Sound offers: genuine, enforced, uncompromising privacy that no amount of resort-style architecture can replicate.

Old Money · Forbes 400 · Industrial Dynasties

Generational Wealth & America's Old-Money Families

Hobe Sound has been a sanctuary for America's generational-wealth families since the early 20th century — long before Palm Beach became the celebrity address it is today. DuPont family members, Dodge heirs, and other industrial dynasties established estates in Hobe Sound precisely because it was quiet, natural, and off the social circuit. That tradition continues: Forbes 400 members, trust-fund family offices, and multigenerational wealth families own here because the community culture has never changed. These properties often pass from generation to generation entirely off-market, sometimes through estate planning and family trusts that never result in a public listing. This is Old Florida — and Old Money — at its most authentic.

PGA Tour Professionals · Medalist Members

Tour Players at The Medalist

Beyond Tiger, The Medalist Golf Club's invitation-only membership has historically included multiple PGA Tour professionals who train and socialize at the club. The combination of a world-class course (Pete Dye and Greg Norman's masterpiece), strict privacy enforcement, and a membership cap of approximately 150 creates an environment where the world's best golfers can practice without photographers, fans, or media. Medalist has hosted informal pro-am events and practice sessions that have never been publicized. Its 18 holes are considered among the most challenging in Florida — which is exactly why elite players who want to be tested choose it.

Wall Street South · Finance Elite · Private Buyers

The New Wave: Finance & Tech Buyers

As Wall Street has migrated to Palm Beach County, Hobe Sound has emerged as the destination of choice for finance professionals who want maximum privacy at a measurable discount to Jupiter Island. Hedge fund managers, private equity partners, and senior Goldman Sachs and Blackstone executives are increasingly exploring Hobe Sound as a genuine alternative. The combination of Martin County's lower tax rates, the National Wildlife Refuge as a permanent natural buffer, and waterfront property prices often 20–40% below equivalent Palm Beach County addresses creates a compelling financial case alongside the lifestyle appeal.

Fishing Legends · Blue-Water Captains · Anglers

The Fishing Community: World-Class Sport Fishermen

Hobe Sound is quietly regarded as one of the top sportfishing addresses in America. The Indian River Lagoon — which runs the entire length of Hobe Sound's waterfront — is a UNESCO-recognized biodiversity hotspot and one of the most productive snook, redfish, and tarpon fisheries in the United States. Combined with quick ocean access via the St. Lucie Inlet to the north and Jupiter Inlet to the south, Hobe Sound gives serious anglers two-inlet access to the Gulf Stream. Professional fishing captains, tournament anglers, and serious offshore fishermen own here specifically for this combination. Several world-record sailfish and tarpon have been caught from Hobe Sound–based vessels.

Hobe Sound Lifestyle

Nature, privacy, water, and an authentically Floridian pace of life

  • Pristine Nature & WildlifeThe Hobe Sound National Wildlife Refuge protects 1,035 acres of barrier island beach — one of the most important loggerhead sea turtle nesting beaches in the US. The Jonathan Dickinson State Park (10,000 acres) is minutes away for hiking, kayaking, and wildlife viewing.
  • World-Class FishingThe Loxahatchee River, Indian River Lagoon, and easy Atlantic Ocean access via the Hobe Sound Inlet make this one of Florida's premier sportfishing destinations. Snook, tarpon, permit, and offshore pelagics are all minutes away.
  • Deep-Water BoatingMultiple communities offer deep-water dockage on the Intracoastal, Indian River Lagoon, and tidal creeks. Cruising access to the Bahamas and the Keys is straightforward via Lake Worth or St. Lucie Inlets.
  • The Medalist Golf Club — One of the world's truly elite private golf experiences. Greg Norman & Pete Dye design. Elite PGA Tour training destination. Membership is by invitation only. Owning a home on the Medalist grounds is the ultimate statement of golf-lifestyle commitment.
  • Lower Martin County TaxesMartin County's property tax rates and general cost of living are lower than Palm Beach County's. For buyers considering the Jupiter Island / Hobe Sound market, the Martin County side offers meaningful tax savings over equivalent Palm Beach County properties.
  • Authentic Old Florida CharacterHobe Sound resists overdevelopment. There are no high-rises, no resort hotel strips, and no theme parks. Downtown Hobe Sound (SE Federal Hwy) has a handful of excellent restaurants, galleries, and boutiques — the kind of genuine small-town character that has largely disappeared from coastal South Florida.
  • Privacy Without PretensionJupiter Island is known for its extreme privacy but also its formidable prices and restrictive access. Hobe Sound gives you much of the same privacy, waterfront character, and Old Florida beauty at prices that are often 20–40% lower — with lower taxes to boot.
  • Access to Palm Beach CountyJupiter is 10–15 minutes south; Palm Beach Gardens is 25 minutes; West Palm Beach is 40 minutes. Palm Beach International Airport is 40–45 minutes. Hobe Sound's location is well-positioned for Palm Beach County commuters who want a quieter home base.

Hobe Sound FAQ

Most common questions from buyers exploring this hidden gem

🌿 How private is Hobe Sound compared to Jupiter Island?

Hobe Sound offers comparable levels of privacy to Jupiter Island without the Town of Jupiter Island's formal access controls. The National Wildlife Refuge and lack of commercial development create an organic privacy buffer. Most luxury buyers find Hobe Sound feels equally private — just more relaxed and accessible than the extremely formal Jupiter Island environment.

⛵ What fishing and boating is available from Hobe Sound?

Hobe Sound is considered one of the finest fishing locations in the state. The Indian River Lagoon is a UNESCO-recognized biodiversity hotspot for sportfish. The Loxahatchee River provides snook and tarpon fishing minutes from home docks. The Hobe Sound Inlet provides quick Atlantic Ocean access for offshore fishing — blackfin tuna, mahi, sailfish, and wahoo are all realistic targets. For cruising, Jupiter Inlet (10 min south) or St. Lucie Inlet (20 min north) provide Gulf Stream access.

🏌️ How do you get into The Medalist Golf Club?

The Medalist is strictly by invitation only and is capped at approximately 150 members. Membership cannot be purchased — it must be sponsored by an existing member and approved by the club. Owning a home within the Medalist community does not automatically confer membership. If Medalist membership is a goal, working with a highly connected agent who has relationships within the club is essential. Carla Christenson's 29 years of Palm Beach County connections may be helpful in this regard.

🏡 Is Hobe Sound mostly seasonal or year-round?

Hobe Sound has a mix of year-round residents and seasonal snowbirds. Unlike some South Florida communities that feel deserted in summer, Hobe Sound's year-round community is active and engaged. The National Wildlife Refuge, fishing, and natural outdoor activities appeal to residents year-round. Summer in Hobe Sound is quieter and more private — many residents consider it the best time of year.

📊 Is now a good time to buy in Hobe Sound?

The Martin County waterfront market has seen significant appreciation over the past five years, and Hobe Sound in particular remains undervalued relative to its Palm Beach County neighbors to the south. As Palm Beach and Jupiter prices have pushed buyers to explore alternatives, Hobe Sound has gained attention from sophisticated buyers who understand the value equation. The limited inventory of waterfront properties and the National Wildlife Refuge's permanent protection of the coastline make this a fundamentally supply-constrained market. Acting before further price discovery occurs is advisable for motivated buyers.

Hobe Sound's Extraordinary Lifestyle

Why the world's most private buyers have chosen this coastline for generations

The Medalist Golf Club

The Medalist Golf Club is one of the most exclusive private golf addresses in the world. Co-designed by Greg Norman and Pete Dye, it is a world-renowned training destination for elite PGA Tour professionals. Membership is capped at approximately 150 — by invitation only — and the approximately 75 estate homes on the grounds are among the most coveted in Florida. The course is legendary in golf circles for its difficulty, conditioning, and the caliber of its membership. Simply being associated with The Medalist carries a prestige that no amount of money can buy without an invitation.

Hobe Sound National Wildlife Refuge

The 1,035-acre Hobe Sound National Wildlife Refuge is Hobe Sound's greatest asset and its most powerful development barrier. Protected by the federal government, it ensures that the miles of pristine barrier island beach and estuarine habitat immediately adjacent to Hobe Sound's most exclusive estates will never be developed. This is not a park that might one day be rezoned — it is permanently protected federal wilderness. For buyers who value the natural environment as much as the built one, this protection is invaluable and irreplaceable.

World-Class Fishing & Boating

Hobe Sound's position on the Indian River Lagoon — one of the most biodiverse estuaries in North America — gives residents extraordinary fishing and boating access. Snook, redfish, tarpon, and trout dominate the inshore fishery. Offshore access via St. Lucie Inlet to the north and Jupiter Inlet to the south puts blue-water fishing within easy reach. Sailfish, wahoo, and mahi-mahi are regular catches. The Intracoastal Waterway runs the full length of Hobe Sound, giving waterfront homeowners quiet cruising access in both directions.

Martin County Tax Advantage

Hobe Sound sits in Martin County, which has historically maintained lower property tax rates than neighboring Palm Beach County. Combined with Florida's zero state income tax, Hobe Sound buyers enjoy a fiscal environment that keeps more money in their pockets than comparable properties to the south. On a $5M Hobe Sound estate, the Martin County property tax advantage over an equivalent Palm Beach County address can represent tens of thousands in annual savings — year after year.

Old Money & Generational Wealth

Hobe Sound has long been a sanctuary for Forbes 400 members, generational-wealth families, and prominent figures from finance, industry, and American history who have valued absolute privacy over decades. Many of its most exclusive properties have passed through the same family networks for 50+ years, rarely appearing on the public market. This is a community with deep institutional memory — where who you know matters more than how much you offer, and where relationships and reputation are currency that money alone cannot buy.

Old Florida Character & Natural Beauty

There is no commercial tourist strip in Hobe Sound, no hotel district, and no chain-restaurant row. What you find instead is a genuine small-town character that South Florida largely abandoned decades ago — shaded streets, independent businesses, a beloved local diner, and neighbors who know each other by name. For buyers who grew up in authentic small communities and miss that quality, Hobe Sound is extraordinarily rare. Its Old Florida character is preserved not by accident but by the deliberate choices of its residents to protect what makes it special.

Hobe Sound and Jupiter Island share a zip code — 33455 — but the more useful thing they share is a residential logic. Both communities developed around the same underlying values: low density, limited commercial intrusion, a governance structure that prioritized preservation over growth, and buyers who tend to value privacy over visibility. The zip code is a postal convenience. The shared logic is the reason certain buyers end up in one or the other, and occasionally in both.

The distinction between them is worth understanding. Hobe Sound has community — a downtown that still functions as one, neighbors who interact, an identity that includes rather than excludes. Jupiter Island, by contrast, becomes quieter still: a barrier island a few hundred feet wide in sections, two bridges in, no commercial development, and a market where many transactions happen quietly. The island is not better or worse than Hobe Sound. It simply moves further toward privacy, separation, and minimal interruption. Some buyers look at Hobe Sound, appreciate everything about it, and then ask whether anything exists with less. Jupiter Island is the answer to that question.

The two places form a corridor that is worth considering as a whole before a search narrows. Buyers drawn to this part of the coast by the Martin County tax structure, the natural environment, the fishing, or the general absence of South Florida's performative energy tend to find that Hobe Sound and Jupiter Island together account for most of what they were looking for — distributed across two different kinds of properties and two different rhythms of daily life.
